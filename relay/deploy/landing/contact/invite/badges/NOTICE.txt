Store badges
============

Google Play
-----------

Localized "Get it on Google Play" SVGs vendored from
https://github.com/steverichey/google-play-badge-svg
(img/fr-ca_get.svg, en_get.svg, es_get.svg).

That repository packages badges derived from Google's promotional assets
under an MIT license for the packaging; Google Play and the Google Play logo
are trademarks of Google LLC. See Google's brand guidelines before altering
these files:
https://developer.android.com/distribute/marketing-tools/brand-guidelines

  google-play-fr-ca.svg  — Français (Canada)
  google-play-en.svg     — English
  google-play-es.svg     — Español
