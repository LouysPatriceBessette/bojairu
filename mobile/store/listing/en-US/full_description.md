IMPORTANT — CLOSED TEST IN SIMULATION MODE

This test build starts in Simulation mode and stays there. That is intentional: it is the framework for closed testing.

Why this is required
• You do not need a roommate, a partner, or a second phone to test. Simulated participants stand in for other people.
• This test is meant to exercise Housing and Contacts solo, without depending on another real person being available.
• Vehicle and Vehicle sharing are disabled here. If they do nothing: that is not a bug — they are out of scope for this test.
• Real multi-device sync (relay) is not what this track is for. Do not leave the test thinking “someone is missing”: Simulation mode is the intended way to explore.

Before you write to support or abandon
• Orange “Simulation” ribbon = you are in solo test mode.
• Typical path: create / open a housing plan → participants → Simulation mode (already on in this build) → explore with simulated participants.
• Report real issues on Housing / Contacts / simulation. Do not report Vehicle / Sharing as “broken.”

HOUSING (focus of this test)
• Build the plan before committing: recurring and one-off expenses, configurable splits, agreement terms
• Propose the plan; each participant (including simulated ones) accepts, negotiates, or refuses
• The agreement becomes active only when everyone has accepted the same revision
• Keep the ledger: expenses, payments, automatic balances, reminders, and budget alerts

CONTACTS (focus of this test)
• One people list reused across modules
• In Simulation mode: simulated participants so you can test without a real invite
• Outside simulation (later builds): connect via invitation code — Bojairũ does not access your phone’s address book

VEHICLE AND SHARING (out of scope for this test)
Coming later in a build not limited to Simulation mode. In this test build: deliberately disabled.

PRIVACY
Bojairũ is local-first: your agreements and accounts live first on your device. Outside simulation, sync between participants uses an ephemeral encrypted relay — without a cloud that can read your personal data. In Simulation mode, work stays on-device with simulated participants (no real human-to-human relay traffic).
